PlayDex - Personal Milestones Ideas
==================================

Milestone substeps
- Break a checkpoint into smaller tasks, with progress such as 3 of 5 completed.

Pin your next milestone
- Show one chosen checkpoint on the active-session card and weekly rotation row.

Reusable templates
- Save milestone sets, such as Finish campaign / Complete side quests / Find collectibles, and apply them to other games.

Groups and manual ordering
- Organize milestones under groups such as Campaign, Challenges, or Collectibles, and choose their order.

Completion timeline
- Show recently completed personal milestones across all games in Profile, with dates and game artwork.

Numeric progress
- Track goals such as 12 of 30 collectibles, with a progress bar.

Milestone dependencies
- Make milestones such as Finish Chapter 2 available after completing Finish Chapter 1.

Optional target dates
- Set a date to aim for and highlight approaching milestones.

Completion prompts after sessions
- When stopping a game, optionally ask whether any pending milestones were completed.

Repeatable milestones
- Reset selected challenges weekly or monthly while keeping their completion history.
