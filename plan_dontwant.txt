PlayDex - Rejected Ideas
========================

Ideas listed here are not wanted for this project. Do not propose or implement them unless the user explicitly changes their decision.

Session enjoyment rating
- Optional 1-5 session ratings and enjoyment comparisons alongside playtime.

Session notes
- Optional session notes and the Continue where I left off note previews beside start actions.

Backlog picks
- A Want to play list and a From my backlog option in What should I play?

Repeatable milestones
- Reset selected challenges weekly or monthly while keeping their completion history.

DLC support
- Child DLC records, content switching, split-time sessions, and independent DLC milestones or HLTB progress.
